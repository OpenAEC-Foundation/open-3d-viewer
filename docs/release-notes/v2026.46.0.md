# open-3d-viewer 2026.46.0

Open 3D Viewer Studio, with the Viewer, the Modeller and the BIM Collaborator behind the switch at
the top right of the window.

## Drawings

- Floor plans, sections and elevations of any model, made per level and cut through the model,
  with dimensions, texts, grid lines and levels.
- Export of a view or a sheet to DWG and DXF (through the acadrust codec), IFCCAD, PDF, PNG and
  SVG ([#100](https://github.com/OpenAEC-Foundation/open-3d-viewer/issues/100)).
- PDF and CAD drawings as underlays on a level.

## Revit files

- Plans, sections, legends and drafting views as the file draws them: texts, dimensions, tags with
  the labels of their family, detail components, filled regions, hatching and line colours.
- Sheets with their viewports, title block (lines, fills, texts and images), view titles and the
  schedules placed on them.
- Schedules as tables, also exported to Excel.
- The views in the explorer in the folders of the file's project browser.

## The explorer and the 3D view

- The explorer lists each model with its views, schedules, sheets, legends and levels under it;
  a click on a model shows its project data.
- Each model shows its zero point; a model can be moved, and its zero point moved within it.
- A floor plan or section can be laid in the 3D view in full.
- IFC: the georeference is read; a map of the place is shown on request.

## The BIM Collaborator

- Issues in BCF 2.0, 2.1 and 3.0; information checks with IDS, the built-in model checks and the
  NL BIM basis ILS; clash tests; comparison of two versions of a model.

## The Modeller

- Timber frame walls with their studs, plates and openings, and the joins at corners and tees.
- A placement strip with the type and sizes of the next element; grips on walls.
- Walls of a Revit file can be edited; wall openings reach to the edge; joins are made as elements meet.
- End plates on steel members.

## More formats

- Blender and FreeCAD files; Archicad layouts as sheets.
