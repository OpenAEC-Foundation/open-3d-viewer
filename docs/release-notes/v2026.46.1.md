# open-3d-viewer 2026.46.1

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet) and the BIM Collaborator
behind the switch at the top right of the window. The suite has a new name and a new logo: three
storeys, one for each product.

## Revit files

- Files open in seconds; the drawings of a large project are read six times as fast, and the
  views of its sheets are cut ahead in the background, behind the window.
- 3D views show as they are set up: what they leave out (categories, filters, phase,
  discipline), how they draw the rest (line colours, halftone, transparency, the colour of a
  solid surface pattern) and their visual style. The camera frames what the view shows.
- A floor plan, section, elevation or detail chosen in the list of views opens as its drawing,
  as its sheets show it. A view's tab draws on paper, the cut in ink; "As on sheet" switches back
  to the model, "In 3D" lays the view in the 3D view where it is taken.
- On sheets: filled regions, masking regions and detail components cover the model beneath them;
  grids with their bubbles in plans and sections; split views in their pieces; view filters and
  view templates set hatching, line colour and pen; viewport titles keep their typeface; tabs in
  text notes; dimensions in the format of their type; areas of floors, roofs and ceilings in
  schedules; piles in foundation plans; phasing.
- Sheets of older files show their title block.

## Other formats

- Coordination files of a model checker (.smc) open, with their issues; they load in a quarter
  of the time and cut nearly all their openings.
- A level keeps its path when an IFCX project is opened again.

## The 3D view

- Ambient shadows in the shaded styles: surfaces are darker in corners, under overhangs and
  behind what stands in front. "Shadows" in the Display group switches them off.
- The view cube offers wireframe, hidden line and shaded. Wireframe and hidden line no longer
  make the window wait, and wireframe draws the edges of the elements instead of every triangle.
- W, A, S and D walk through the model in the Viewer; Q and E go down and up; faster with Shift.
- Large models turn at 120 frames a second and more; the section box moves at once while its cut
  faces follow; a section fills the earth under the terrain.

## The interface

- Ten languages: Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Swedish
  and Turkish.
- The ribbon fits the window: on a narrow window its groups show icons only. The settings have a
  Ribbon page to choose which groups show, and a text size beside the interface size, as in the
  current OpenAEC Blueprint.
- A Feedback button in the title bar sends what you write, and the version of the app, to the
  feedback service the OpenAEC apps share. Nothing of the model is sent.
- The title bar names the products to come: FM, Analyse and Environment.

## The Modeller (alpha)

- Undo and redo; doors and windows in timber frames; move, copy, rotate and mirror; metal stud
  partitions; floors and ceilings as systems; joined elements without the seam between them.
