# open-3d-viewer 2026.46.3

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Collaborator and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet)
behind the switch at the top right of the window. On top of [2026.46.2](v2026.46.2.md):

## The BIM Collaborator

- Clashes are grouped and remembered between runs (new, solved), with a discipline matrix,
  tolerances per pair of disciplines, a storey as scope, sight-line clashes and triage with keys;
  a test runs again when a model changes.
- Colour by property, with selection sets; a takeoff of quantities, also exported to Excel.
- Checks of rooms, doors, escape routes and fire compartments; a room's walk to its way out is
  drawn when its finding is picked.
- Reports in house style as PDF; issues written as BCF 3.0; a project shared through a folder
  (issues and clash decisions); a project's own rules; a rule's class takes its properties from
  the buildingSMART Data Dictionary.
- In the 3D view: the distances between grid lines, the levels with their marks, and grid heads
  behind the model left out; a scan can be laid onto the model.

## The Modeller (alpha)

- Undo and redo also after editing a wall of a Revit file.
- Connections between building systems, such as timber frame walls: butt or apart.
- Grid lines in 2D; the explorer opens on the Project tab.
