# open-3d-viewer 2026.46.4

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Collaborator and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet)
behind the switch at the top right of the window. This release also carries what
[2026.46.3](v2026.46.3.md) announced, which was not published. On top of that:

## Revit files

- Tags write their text again in files of 2024 and before: door and window marks, reinforcement
  tags, and room tags with their number, name and use.
- In a plan whose crop is turned, a tag set level or upright lies so on the sheet.

## The Viewer

- The bar over a view offers "As on sheet", "In 3D" and "Export" only; dimensioning, writing
  and cutting stay with the BIM Collaborator and the Modeller.
- A "3D" button in the Camera group always goes back to the 3D view.
- The pointer over the 3D view is the arrow.
- "Views in 3D" is on while a view lies in the model; switching it off takes them all away.

## Export

- A sheet of a Revit file exports to DWG, DXF and the other drawing formats, with its title
  block and the views it places.
- The export of a view has its detail lines, on a layer of their own.

## The browser version

- Builds again, without the parts of the BIM Collaborator that need the desktop.
