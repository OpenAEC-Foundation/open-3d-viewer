# open-3d-viewer 2026.46.5

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Collaborator and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet)
behind the switch at the top right of the window. On top of [2026.46.4](v2026.46.4.md):

## The 3D view

- Ambient shadows are off by default. "Show ambient shadows" under 3D view in the settings
  switches them on, and the choice is kept; "Shadows" in the ribbon still switches them at once.
- A new project tab takes the settings of the 3D view from the project shown.
