# open-3d-viewer 2026.47.1

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Collaborator, Pointcloud (alpha) and the Modeller (alpha,
not usable yet) behind the switch at the top right of the window. On top of
[2026.46.5](v2026.46.5.md):

## Pointcloud, a fourth product (alpha, desktop)

- Point clouds (LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, PTS, PTX, PCD, XYZ) open in a product of their own, with a
  ribbon tab for what is done with them. Its jobs are done by the point-cloud core of Open
  Pointcloud Studio and read the cloud's own file, not the thinned points of the view.
- A scan project file (`.rcp`) opens as the scans it lists that lie beside it, and a folder as
  the scans in it. The indexed copies of a project (`.rcs`) are a closed format and are not read.
- The points are coloured by their stored colour, their elevation, their intensity or their class
  or scan, shaded by depth, and drawn a step smaller or larger.
- Three meshes of a cloud: a terrain mesh, a surface mesh and a closed mesh, with their settings
  (vertices, voxel, neighbours, longest edge, gaps to close, share of the points). A mesh tells its
  open edges and loose parts, a closed mesh how far the points lie from it.
- "Faces" finds the walls, floors, ceilings and sloped faces and the round columns and pipes in
  the points.
- The points are exported as LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, XYZ, PTS or CSV: all, a share, or what lies in
  the section box. The mesh made last is exported as OBJ, PLY, STL, DXF, DWG or IFC.
- "Section drawing" draws the points in a slab at a face of the section box as DXF or DWG.
- "Details" shows what a file holds: points, size, attributes, scanner stations and photos.
- With the section box on, the meshes, the faces, the export and the drawing cover what lies in
  it. A running job can be stopped.

## Viewer

- The views of a Revit file can be ordered by each organization of its browser, chosen above
  the views in the explorer. The version stands in the title of the window.
- A tag's properties name what it tags, also in older files, and a room tag its room.
- A building pad cuts its hole out of the terrain, also in a section.
- Structural analysis models in a text format (`.e2k`) open.
- The drawings of a Vertex project show in the explorer, with the colours of their lines and the
  look of their texts; their parts are picked one by one and show what the reader knows of them.
- An IFCCAD drawing is exported as `.ifccad`; a sheet's export draws the lines of its views and
  keeps the alignment of its texts.
- The window is resized by its edges and keeps its size between displays.

## BIM Collaborator

- Room walks, checks on any model, clash words, a house style and a BCF server.

## Modeller (alpha, not usable yet)

- A Select tool with a selection box, letters that start tools, and keys as in other modellers.
- Walls between levels, a rectangle of four walls with two clicks, layers and materials kept from
  a file and written to IFC 4; doors and windows as elements of their own, with a frame.
- A slab is a floor, a roof or a ceiling, drawn by a sketch with lines, rectangles, arcs and
  openings, with grips on its edges; sloped slabs.
- The walls of a level close in spaces, named and measured, listed with their areas.
- Elements are picked, gripped and moved in floor plans, sections and elevations; temporary
  dimensions move a wall by a typed value; a dimension holds on to what it was clicked on.
- A model explorer, a project browser, a 3D cursor, and a library of types a project can add.
- Lines, rectangles and circles are drawn on a view.

## Automation

- The MCP drives the Modeller, clicks any point of the window, and lists and opens the project's
  own drawings.
