# open-3d-viewer 2026.47.2

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Validator, Docs, Pointcloud (alpha), Structural Analysis
(alpha) and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet) behind the switch at the top right of the window.
On top of [2026.47.1](v2026.47.1.md):

## The BIM Validator (was: the BIM Collaborator)

- The product is now called the BIM Validator. A product saved as `collaborator` in the
  preferences and `--product collaborator` still work; the program of this product alone is
  `open-bim-validator`.
- BCF files go to and from other parties. Opening a BCF file while the project has issues takes
  its issues in with them: new ones are added, and of an issue both have the later change stands,
  with the comments of both. "Send BCF" writes the issues for a party, the open ones only unless
  asked otherwise. The Issues tab lists the files taken in and sent.
- A model is checked from the command line, without a window:
  `open-3d-viewer FILE.ifc --check OUT.html|json|csv [--ids FILE.ids] [--rules NAME] [--issues OUT.bcf]`.
  It writes the report and an issue per failed rule, and ends with code 1 when a rule failed.

## Docs, a new product

- The documents of the project folder in a product of its own, with a register kept in IFCX.

## Structural Analysis, a new product (alpha)

- Draws the loads and results of an analysis model in 3D.
- Analysis results in IFC (`.ifcfemresults`) open with their forces and displacements, the
  internal forces in the model's own signs.
- The deformed shape is drawn with its profiles, with solid arrows and supports.

## Viewer

- **Rhino (.3dm)**: polysurfaces, extrusions and SubD without a saved mesh are meshed by the
  viewer; curves, texts, hatches, dimensions and leaders, linetypes and text dots are drawn;
  clipping planes open as the section of the model; named views and hidden objects are kept;
  Rhino 1 files open; a Rhino model saves as IFC.
- **Tekla**: levels, grid lines, saved views and drawings as sheets; bolts, items, polybeams,
  welds, reinforcement bars with their hooks and reinforcing meshes; models of Tekla
  Structures 2026.
- **Vectorworks (.vwx, .sta)**: the model, its drawings, styles and document data.
- **IFCX** is read through `ifcx-core`; files of IFCX Explorer open.
- **GIS**: a place is found by its address, with the parcels and buildings around it.
- **IFC 4x3**: any model saves as IFC 4x3, written by the viewer itself
  (`--save-as OUT.ifc --schema IFC4X3`).
- A Blender scene in millimetres opens at its size.
- Fitting the view leaves out a small thing far from the model.
- The product switch has short names; products in alpha are grey.

## Under the bonnet

- Every file format's reader moves to a repository of its own: the Rhino reader, and of Revit
  the joins, images, bearings, materials and phases.
- Each release is also copied to `open-3d-viewer-studio`.
- CI passes each private reader its own deploy key, and names the keys it passes.
