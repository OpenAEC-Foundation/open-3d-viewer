# open-3d-viewer 2026.47.4

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Validator, Docs, Pointcloud (alpha), Structural Analysis
(alpha) and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet) behind the switch at the top right of the window.
On top of [2026.47.3](v2026.47.3.md):

## SolidWorks files open (beta)

- A SolidWorks part (`.sldprt`) and assembly (`.sldasm`) open as a 3D model, on the desktop and in
  the browser. The viewer shows what SolidWorks itself saved for showing the model: its faces as
  triangles, in the colours of the faces and bodies, every part of an assembly in its place. Each
  part is a layer.
- Like every model, it saves as IFC 4, IFC 4x3 and IFCX.
- Read are the compound files SolidWorks wrote up to at least 2016. A file in the newer container
  is refused with a message; it has not been seen yet. Send one through an issue.
- Not read: the exact geometry (only the saved tessellation, for the configuration the file was
  saved in), textures and transparency, the feature tree, mates and custom properties. Hidden and
  suppressed components are not told apart from shown ones. A part an assembly holds no
  tessellation of is read from the part file next to it, which the browser cannot do.
- Checked on one design: the 110 files of the BCN3D Moveo robot arm (87 parts, 23 assemblies)
  all open. Other designs and versions may lose colours or shared parts.
- The reader lives in its own repository, `solidworks-dll`.
