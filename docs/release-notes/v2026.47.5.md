# open-3d-viewer 2026.47.5

Open BIM Suite, with the Viewer, the BIM Validator, Docs, GIC, Pointcloud (alpha), Structural
Analysis (alpha) and the Modeller (alpha, not usable yet) behind the switch at the top right of the
window. On top of [2026.47.4](v2026.47.4.md):

## Models of one building laid on each other by their grids

- When a second model is added and both have grids, it is moved in plan so that the grids of the
  same name lie on each other. The status bar says by how many grids and how far, and the move is
  the model's own move in the right pane, where it can be changed.
- Grids of one name that do not agree are left out and counted. Models whose grids run in other
  directions are not turned and not moved.

## BIM Validator: steel around frames

- A new kind of clash test, **Frame steel**: per window and door it looks for a post at each side,
  a lintel above and, under a window, a sill member, within 250 mm of the frame. It finds the
  steel that is missing, not only what crosses.
- The built-in rule set **Steel around frames** takes the windows and doors of one model and the
  beams and columns of another, in walls whose type or name holds "sandwich". The editor's
  **In walls** field sets which walls take part.
- The clash list, CSV, reports, issues and the MCP name what is missing, such as "no: post left,
  lintel". A lintel above a door counts from 2 m above its foot, so doors drawn taller than their
  frame are not flagged.

## GIC: GIS, infrastructure and civil

- GIS becomes **GIC** on the product switch, with the ribbon pages GIS, Infra, Civil, Rail,
  Geotechnics and View (Infra, Civil and Rail hold only the file group yet).
- **Load map** lays the background map, the aerial photograph or a WMS service of one's own under
  the project, on the terrain when there is one. A model on the map turns about its zero point.
- **Geotechnics**: cone penetration tests and borings from the national register of the
  subsurface around the place, or from GEF files, as columns in the colours of the resistance or
  the soil. A first ground model takes the layers of the nearest boring; it is no soil profile to
  calculate with, and the tab says so.

## Formats

- **Solid Edge** (beta): parts (`.par`), sheet metal (`.psm`) and assemblies (`.asm`) open as their
  display tessellation, in the colours of their face styles.
- **DICOM** (`.dcm`): a file that carries an embedded STL or OBJ model opens, on the desktop and in
  the browser. Image series (CT, MRI) are refused with a message saying so.
- **Tekla**: a model folder opens as a whole (drop it, or **Open folder…**). Objects take Tekla's
  own colours and transparency, each view leaves out what its view filter leaves out, and the
  analysis model opens in the analysis view with supports, releases, springs, loads and
  combinations (their factors not yet). Tekla 20 models are read as fully as newer ones.
- **Rhino 3dm**: layouts as sheets with their details as viewports, text dots as labels, lights,
  physically based material colours, texts in the font the file names, and the earth anchor point
  as georeference. IFC export writes the map conversion of every georeferenced model.
- **Revit**: the browser's orders and typefaces are read by the reader's own library; nothing
  changes in what is shown.

## Web app in a SuperCloud environment

- Opened from an environment's Docs, the web app reads the file it was opened for, **Save as**
  stores a new version there, and where the environment offers it, **Render with Blender** renders
  the stored file. Without an environment the web app and the desktop app work as before.
