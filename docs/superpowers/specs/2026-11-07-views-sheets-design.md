# Views, sheets and dimensions

**Status:** built in small steps on 2026-11-07, each shown to the project owner in the running app; follows [levels](2026-11-07-levels-design.md).

## Intent

After levels, the project owner wants to make drawings in the viewer: views of the model (floor plans per level first), sheets with those views placed on them, views moved over a sheet, and dimensions. Within one project there are several views, each in its own tab; selecting in one view selects in all of them. Everything lives in the IfcX project document, which will be saved as `.ifcx` later.

## What exists

- **Floor plans** (`document::PlanView`): made per level in the explorer's **Views** tab, named after the level (`<level> (2)` for a second one). A plan looks from 0.3 m under its level up to a cut 1.2 m above it. A plan goes with its level.
- **View tabs** (`app::DocTab`): the pinned 3D view, then the open floor plans, schedules, Revit sheets and document sheets. Each floor plan tab keeps its own camera and section box (`ViewSlot`), restored when its tab shows again; the 3D view keeps its own the same way. The selection belongs to the project, so it is shared by all views. In a floor plan a left drag pans instead of orbiting. The shell only draws the tabs the app lists.
- **Sheets** (`document::Sheet`): A3 landscape, numbered `A01`, `A02`, ...; each opens in its own tab. The bar above the paper places any floor plan in the middle at 1:100. A placed plan is drawn as the line segments where its cut height meets its own layers (`drawing::cut_at`, computed once per plan and cached in the app). A drag moves a placed view; it stays on the paper.
- **Dimensions** (`document::Dimension`): the **Dimension** tool of the sheet bar; two clicks on a placed plan, snapping to the ends of its cut lines within 8 px, add a dimension to the plan in model coordinates, so it shows wherever the plan is placed and moves with it. Drawn 6 mm off the measured points with extension lines, slashes and the length in millimetres. Escape drops a first point, then switches the tool off.
- **View tabs**: floor plans, sections, elevations and sheets open as document tabs of the application's own window, next to the 3D view, on the desktop as in the browser; a view's tab keeps its camera and section box while another tab shows. A window of their own beside the application was tried and dropped: the views belong in the application.
- **Editing on the sheet**: a click picks a placed view or a dimension. A picked view gets its scale (1:20 to 1:1000), a floor plan its cut height (cut again; its tab's section box follows), a handle on each side of its frame to crop it (`PlanView::crop`, in drawing coordinates; the drawing stays where it is, its tab's box follows), and Take off sheet; Delete removes what is picked. The paper is A4 to A0, landscape.
- **Lines below the cut**: the outlines and creases (over 20°) of the triangles between a plan's view depth and its cut, seen from above, thin and grey under the cut (`drawing::plan_at`; edges matched by their ends rounded to 0.1 mm). About 5 ms for a 46k-triangle model.
- **Sections and elevations** (`document::SectionView`): a line in plan looking to its left, cut along it, seen to a depth beyond it; drawing coordinates are u along the line and v up. **Make four elevations** puts one 2 m outside the model on every side; the sheet's **Section** tool draws one with two clicks on a placed floor plan (named Section A, B, ..), and every placed plan shows its line dash-dot with letter and arrows. Their drawing (`drawing::section_at`) keeps of the edges beyond the line only what no nearer face covers, through a 2048-pixel depth image of the faces; about 60 ms for an elevation of a 46k-triangle model. They open like plans (level, in their box) and take dimensions and crops.
- **Dimensions and texts in a view's tab** (`app::marks`): a bar over the viewport with Dimension, Text and Standard dimensions, and a canvas layer that projects the view's dimensions and texts with the tab's camera and its own size. A view's drawing lies in the model as a `DrawingFrame` (a plan: x, y at its cut; a section: u along its line, v up); the pointer's ray meets that plane. The layer takes a click only for its tools and for a dimension or text under the pointer, and never changes the cursor otherwise, so the viewport keeps zooming and panning. Texts are `document::Note` (a point and a wording) per view, saved in its IfcX node; Delete reaches the app through its key subscription only when no text field took the key.
- **Standard dimensions**: a view's overall width below and depth (plan) or height (section) on the left, outside its cut's extent; grids (plan: those parallel to x or y; section: where they cross its line) and, in a section, the levels divide a side into a chain, and the overall dimension goes 1 m further out. Nothing is made twice.
- **Dragging a view onto a sheet**: a press on a view in the Views list takes it (a `mouse_area` inside the row's button, so the click is its release); the sheet cuts it at once and draws its frame at 1:100 under the pointer, and a release over the paper places it there. The app's subscription lets go on any release.
- **Grids from IFC** (`loaders::ifc::grid_lines`): every `IfcGrid`'s U, V and W axes, polylines and trimmed lines, through the grid's placement chain (IFClite's resolver), at the level nearest the building's zero, as the Revit loader does.
- **Grids and levels per view** (`App::datums`): the viewport draws the datum lines where the view shown has them, the renderer (dash-dot, with depth) and the overlay (heads) from the same list. The 3D view: the model's grids where they are. A floor plan: the grids 0.05 m above its cut, so nothing hides them. A section or elevation: the grids it crosses (within their length and its crop), upright from the bottom to the top of the model and its levels, and the document's levels across the model's width along it, 1 m past it on both sides (or its crop), all 0.05 m in front of its cut. A level's head is a triangle onto the line with its name above and its height below; from the top down a level closer than 28 px below the last named one keeps only its mark.
- **Picking grids** (`app::grids`, `annotations::grid_at`): a click takes a grid before a structural node or member and an element: inside one of its bubbles (drawn on top, so always), or within 5 px of its line where no triangle under the pointer lies in front of the line's point there by more than 1 % of the view distance (the same test as for structural members; the point on the line is found perspective-correct from the screen). A grid is picked alone: any change of the element selection lets it go, as do Escape and switching the grid lines off. The overlay draws it over in the accent colour; the properties pane shows its name, length, direction, elevation and ends; the remote `pick` names it.
- **Layers per view** (`app::layers`, `document::LayerSwitches`): the layers moved from the explorer to the right pane, which has two tabs, **Properties** and **Layers**. The Layers tab switches the layers of the view shown only, with its own search, Show all and Hide all, and the layer groups as before. A floor plan, section or elevation keeps, by layer name, the layers it shows otherwise than the model (`PlanView::layers`, `SectionView::layers`, saved as `layers` in its `o3v::view::` node); the 3D view keeps its own switches in the app while another view shows. A placed view is cut through its own layers and cut again when they change (or a model is switched on or off). On a sheet, the Layers tab shows and switches the placed view picked there. The undo history of what is shown belongs to the view shown. The Properties tab starts, with nothing selected, with the view: its name and kind, a floor plan's level, cut height and view depth, a section's length and depth, and how many layers it switches otherwise than the model. The models and their switches stay the project's.
- **View properties typed in** (`app::view_props`): the View section of the Properties tab takes the name of a floor plan, section or elevation, a floor plan's cut height and view depth, and a section's depth (metres, a comma or a point). A value is used as soon as it is valid: the view is cut again where it was cut (on a sheet, for the dimension tool) and its tab's section box follows; the sheet bar's cut height shows the same value. Until then the field keeps what was typed, and Enter shows the value in use again. A name may not be empty or another view's (case does not count); a floor plan looks down to its level at least and its cut stays above its view depth; a section looks beyond its line. This works for the view shown and for a view picked on a sheet. The kind, level, length and own layers are not typed.
- **IFCX** (`export::ifcx`, Save as IFC5/IFCX): the model as IfcX nodes (project, building, the levels as storeys, one node per element with class, name, properties, colour and mesh, placed under its storey), and the document's plans, sections and sheets as `o3v::` nodes, which the IFCX reader keeps and the document takes over when the file is opened again (a plan finds its level by name). A 3.6 MB IFC saves as IFCX in 0.18 s and opens again in 0.03 s.

- **Sheet layout** (`sheet_layout`, `App::arrange_sheet`, `App::fill_sheets`): **Arrange** in a sheet's bar gives its views one scale, the first of the scales (largest drawing first) at which they all fit, and sets them in rows in their order, each row and the rows together centred, a view on the bottom of its row so the names line up. A view's room is its crop (else its whole cut) with the points its dimensions measure, 10 mm around it for its frame and dimension lines and 10 mm under it for its name, 12 mm between views; the sheet's title keeps a band of 14 mm at the bottom of the frame. **Put loose views on sheets** in the Views list puts the views on no sheet on new A3 sheets: the floor plans (from the lowest level up) on sheets named Floor plans, the sections and elevations on sheets named Sections and elevations; per kind the scale at which each fits on a sheet of its own, then as many in their order per sheet as fit. When nothing fits at 1:1000, the views go at 1:1000 in rows from the top, past the paper.
- **Kinds of plan** (`document::PlanKind`, `element_class`, `drawing::ceiling_at`): a level has floor plans, structural plans and ceiling plans, listed apart in the Views tab, each kind with its own **New**, which lists the levels to make one of. A floor plan cuts 1.2 m above its level and looks 0.3 m below it. A structural plan is named `<level> (structure)`, cuts as a floor plan and looks 1 m below its level (`STRUCTURAL_DEPTH`), so the beams under the floor show; in its tab and its drawing it leaves out the elements that carry nothing, worked out per unit once per model: the element's `LoadBearing` property, else its IFC class (IFC, IFCX) or the class the IFC export gives its Revit, Tekla or Archicad category; walls, slabs, columns, beams, members, plates (not curtain panels), footings, piles, roofs, stairs, ramps, bearings, tendons and chimneys carry; a proxy, an element of a format without classes and the rest of a layer stay. This filter is the kind's, not Hide's: Show all does not bring them back, and they are not saved as hidden. A ceiling plan is named `<level> (ceiling)`, cuts 2.3 m above its level (`CEILING_CUT`) and looks up to the next level (else 1 m, `CEILING_DEPTH`); its tab looks from below, which mirrors it, while its drawing is laid out as the floor plan (a reflected ceiling plan): the cut at its cut height and the edges above it. The grid lines lie just under its cut, and its dimensions and texts on it. IFCX keeps the kind (`kind` of the `o3v::view::plan` node); a node without one is a floor plan.
- **Hidden per view** (`PlanView::hidden`, `SectionView::hidden`, `drawing::Shown`): Hide, Isolate and Show all work in the view shown, as its layers do. A floor plan, section or elevation keeps the elements it hides (unit keys, so they stay when a model is added); the 3D view keeps its own in the app while another view shows. Undo goes back through the view shown. A view is cut without the triangles of the elements it hides (by the unit of a triangle's first vertex) and cut again when they change. The Properties tab counts them. In IFCX a view's node names the nodes of the elements it hides in the same file (`hidden`), or, when it hides more than it shows (Isolate), the nodes of those it shows (`shown`); the reader finds them by the `Id` it keeps for an element's node. The rest of a layer of a model of several files is not named in the file.

## Calls made by the builder

- A view on a sheet is a line drawing of the cut, not a picture of the 3D view: the renderer holds one set of uniforms, so it draws one view at a time, and a line drawing is what a sheet needs and what dimensions snap to.
- Floor plans and view tabs share one viewport whose camera and section box are swapped per tab.
- Hidden lines are removed for sections and elevations, not yet for the lines below a floor plan's cut.
- IFCX is the default of Save as (the project owner's call); IFC is the second choice.
- Default cut 1.2 m, view depth 0.3 m below the level, scale 1:100, A3 landscape, dimension text in whole millimetres, horizontal.
- Arranging a sheet gives all its views one scale (as a sheet of floor plans usually has) and keeps their order; a view of another scale is set by hand after it. Filling sheets uses A3, the paper a new sheet gets.
- A new view shows the layers as the model does (as a new view in a BIM modeller without a view template), not as the 3D view shows them at that moment.
- A typed value of a view is used at once, not only on Enter, so its drawing and its tab follow while it is typed; an invalid value waits in its field.
- Layer switches are kept by layer name, so a view keeps them when the model is opened again or another model is added; two layers of the same name switch together in a view.
